{{NAME}} is a personal-finance app for couples and households, used mainly in Brazil. This page explains what information we handle, why, and what choices you have. We keep it short and in plain language.

## What we collect

- **Account details.** The name and password you use to create an account.
- **Financial entries.** The accounts, balances, credit cards, invoices, purchases, installments, recurring bills, budgets and categories you type in or import.
- **Documents you upload.** For example, a credit-card statement PDF you choose to send so that we can read its transactions.
- **Basic technical data.** Information the browser and hosting services normally handle, such as device type, browser and error logs, needed to keep the app working and secure.

We do not ask for bank passwords, and we do not connect to your bank.

## How we use it

- **To provide the service.** Showing your accounts, reports and budgets, and keeping them in sync between devices.
- **To run the AI features.** Reading uploaded documents, extracting transactions, suggesting categories and, where you use it, answering questions about your own finances.
- **To keep things secure.** Detecting problems, preventing misuse and fixing bugs.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

## AI processing

Some features send the content you submit (for example, a statement you upload or a question you ask the assistant) to Anthropic's API, which processes it and returns a result. Anthropic's commercial terms state that data sent through the API is not used to train its models by default. We do not use your data to train AI models.

## Service providers

We rely on a few companies to run the app:

- **Supabase** stores the app's database.
- **Vercel** hosts the web app.
- **Anthropic** processes content for the AI features.

These providers only handle your data to run the service for us.

- confirm whether we hold your data and give you a copy of it (access);

## Security
